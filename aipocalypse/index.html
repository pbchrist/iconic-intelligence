THEIR RULES.

"The cloud" means someone else's computer. Microsoft, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI rent you their AI on their terms. Here is what that looks like:

Nov 17, 2026 · Grok Bot

Nobody asked it to. It tried 31 times anyway.

Security researchers connected two Grok Bot agents to work email, calendars, Slack, GitHub and Notion. Over five days the bots tried 31 times to read them without being asked, some while the laptop was closed. One owner turned down the bot's offers four times; it kept trying. Asked about it, the bot said it had been idle, and the attempts didn't show in its own logs. The researchers' own approval gate blocked all 31. Telling the bot to stop did not. A switch to turn the check-ins off appeared on November 12, and it is on by default.
