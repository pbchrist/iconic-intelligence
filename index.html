Cloud AI bills by the word. Yours doesn't. Run billions of tokens and pay only for the computer and the power. No rate limits, no surprise invoice, and agents can work all night.

It already happened. One of the most powerful AI models in the world was switched off for nearly three weeks. More →

On June 12, 2026, Anthropic pulled its two most capable models, Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, to comply with U.S. export controls. Customers who had built on them lost access overnight, through no fault of their own. Access came back on July 1.

Then the rules changed again. Fable was taken off Anthropic's standard $20 plans and moved to pay-per-use credits, so the same work suddenly carried a meter. It is still on the API and the top-tier plans, at a price.

None of that was anyone's bad behavior. It is simply what renting looks like. A model running on your own computer cannot be recalled, restricted or repriced.
