QUESTIONS

Before you book.

How do I identify AI opportunities in my company?

Look for four things: money that leaks (leads that never become customers, pricing that trails the market), work that repeats (people doing by hand what a system could do), data nobody uses, and decisions made without information you could have had. The evaluation examines each of these in your business and ranks what it finds by value and feasibility.

How do I know whether an AI investment is worthwhile?

By settling three questions before you spend on a build: what the problem costs you today, whether a system can realistically fix it with your data and tools, and what it would take to build and run. The evaluation answers those in writing. Financial impact is estimated only where the evidence supports an estimate.

How can AI help my business make more money?

Usually in one of a few ways: recovering revenue you are already losing, winning customers competitors currently take, removing manual cost, or giving you information early enough to act on. Which of those applies to you, and how much it is worth, is what the evaluation finds out.

What business processes should I automate?

The ones that are frequent, rule-bound, expensive in staff time and low-risk if a person checks the result. The evaluation lists yours, ranks them, and says which are not worth automating.

Is the evaluation free?

No. It is a paid professional service at a fixed $1,000. The substantive analysis is the product, so it is not given away in a sales call. A short fit conversation beforehand costs nothing.

Do I have to hire ICONIC to build what the evaluation recommends?

No. The findings and the roadmap are yours. You can build with ICONIC under a separate agreement, with your own team, with another firm, or not at all.

What if the evaluation finds AI is not the answer?

Then it says so. Some problems are better fixed with a process change, a hire or software you already own. An honest “don’t build this” is a finding worth paying for.
