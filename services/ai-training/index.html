WHAT YOU LEAVE WITH

Skills you can use the same day.

  1. Know which tool to use for which job. And which jobs AI should not be trusted with yet.

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Is this just ChatGPT training?

It starts with what you need AI to do. We can set up basic private AI, teach research and workflows, or test how much intelligence a task requires. More technical topics include Hermes, shared memory, MCP, GitHub, NVIDIA/CUDA, Linux and production systems.

How can a small business use AI safely?

Start by deciding what may and may not go into an AI tool: client files, customer records, pricing and anything privileged or regulated. Then choose tools and settings that match those rules, and make sure everyone on the team knows them. Training covers this for your industry, including when work should stay on a private system instead of a cloud service.

What does AI training for attorneys cover?

AI-assisted legal research, drafting and document analysis, and how to do each without putting client confidences or privileged material at risk. The American Bar Association’s Formal Opinion 512 (July 2024) says lawyers using AI must protect client confidences and understand the tools they use. The training is built around that duty.

What does AI training for recruiters cover?

AI-assisted sourcing, candidate research and intelligence, advanced search and research workflows, recruiting automation and process optimization. ICONIC’s principal spent a decade in technical recruiting before building AI systems.
