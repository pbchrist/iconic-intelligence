QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

How much does custom AI implementation cost?

ICONIC builds start at $20,000. The price of a particular build depends on the business requirements, complexity, the systems it must integrate with, the risk involved and the support it will need. You receive a fixed scope and price before any work begins.

Does every build cost $20,000?

No. $20,000 is the starting point for a production system, not a flat fee. Larger or more integrated systems cost more, and each one is scoped individually.

Does every business need a custom build?

No. Some problems are solved by training your people or by using software you already have. If that is the right answer, ICONIC will say so.

Do I need an evaluation before a build?

Not if you already know what needs to change and can describe it. If you are not yet sure what is worth building, the $2,000 evaluation is the cheaper way to find out.

Can the system run privately?

Yes. ICONIC builds secure, private deployments, including systems that run on a computer in your own office so your files are not sent to an AI company. The live demo runs that way.

How can AI improve dealership profitability?

One working example: an ICONIC agent watches a GMC dealership’s lot and five competitors’ lots every morning, truck by truck, and briefs the owners three times a week. It showed that a rival the owner believed was $500 to $800 cheaper was in fact cheaper by an average of $4,472 on matched trucks, and it caught a pricing error on the dealership’s own website. See the dealership pricing research.

What happens after the build?

You own a working system. ICONIC can keep it monitored, maintained and improving under an ongoing arrangement, priced to scope.
