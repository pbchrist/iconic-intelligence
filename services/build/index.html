QUESTIONS

Before we talk:

How much does custom AI implementation cost?

ICONIC builds start at $10,000. The price of a particular build depends on the business requirements, complexity, the systems it must integrate with, the risk involved and the support it will need. You receive a fixed scope and price before any work begins.

Does every build cost $10,000?

No. $10,000 is the starting point for a production system, not a flat fee. Larger or more integrated systems cost more, and each one is scoped individually.

Does every business need a custom build?

No. Some problems are solved by training your people or by using software you already have. If that is the right answer, ICONIC will say so.

Do I need an evaluation before a build?

Yes. Builds are by invitation, and every one begins with the $1,000 ICONIC Evaluation. The evaluation establishes what is worth building and in what order, so the build is scoped on evidence. If it shows a system worth building, you are invited to go ahead.

Can the system run privately?

Yes. ICONIC builds secure, private deployments, including systems that run on a computer in your own office so your files are not sent to an AI company. The live demo runs that way.

What happens after the build?

You own a working system. ICONIC can keep it monitored, maintained and improving under an ongoing arrangement, priced to scope.
