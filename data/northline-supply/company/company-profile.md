# Company profile: Northline Supply Co.
Date: 2026-01-10

Northline Supply Co. is a plumbing and HVAC supply distributor in Tacoma, Washington, founded in 1988 by Ray Delgado's father. Ray has owned and run it since 2011.

38 employees: counter and will-call staff, two inside sales reps, four drivers, warehouse crew, purchasing (Dale Pruitt), and an office manager (Gina Moss).

Customers: about 420 active trade accounts, mostly plumbing and mechanical contractors in Pierce and south King counties. The top 10 accounts make up 46% of sales.

What we sell: pipe, fittings and valves, water heaters, PEX, HVAC equipment and parts. Will-call counter opens at 6:30 a.m. on weekdays. Delivery trucks load from 7:00 to 9:00 a.m.

Systems: Epicor for orders and inventory, QuickBooks for accounting, Google Workspace for email and files.
