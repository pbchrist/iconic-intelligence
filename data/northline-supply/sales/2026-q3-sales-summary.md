# Q3 2026 sales summary
Date: 2026-10-07

- Revenue: $4.86 million (Q3 2025: $4.71 million), up 3%
- Gross margin: 27.4% (Q3 2025: 28.9%)
- Will-call share of orders: 31%

Margin pressure came from the 4% Cascade water heater cost increase that we did not pass on, and from more freight on small vendor orders.

Top accounts by Q3 sales: Evergreen Mechanical $872,000 (18% of sales), Bayside Plumbing $341,000, Pierce Heating & Air $298,000, Harbor Mechanical $244,000, Moreno Plumbing $187,000.

Watch: Pierce Heating & Air orders fell 15% from Q2 to Q3. Moreno Plumbing will-call orders fell 22% in September.
