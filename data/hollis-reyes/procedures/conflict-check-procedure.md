# Procedure: conflict checks
Date: 2025-02-10

1. Before any new matter, the intake attorney enters the client, every adverse party, and related companies into Clio.
2. Teresa Wu runs the check and circulates hits to all partners.
3. Because the firm represents both employees and employers, check the former employer of any employee client against current employer clients, and their competitors, if a recruiting or hiring request is involved.
4. No work starts until a partner signs off in writing.
5. Re-run the check whenever a new party appears in an existing matter.
