# Firm profile: Hollis & Reyes LLP
Date: 2026-01-15

Hollis & Reyes LLP is a 14-lawyer employment and business litigation firm in downtown Seattle, founded in 2009 by Dana Hollis and Marco Reyes. Priya Natarajan became the third name partner in 2021.

Practice areas: non-compete and trade secret disputes (about 35% of revenue), wage and hour defense, executive employment agreements, and commercial contract litigation. The firm represents both employees and employers, which makes conflict checks critical.

People: 3 partners, 2 of counsel, 7 associates, 2 paralegals, an office manager (Teresa Wu) and a billing coordinator (Sam Ortiz). Associate Jordan Okafor leads non-compete research.

Standard rates for 2026: partners $485 an hour, of counsel $420, associates $290 to $340, paralegals $165.

Technology: Clio for practice management, Microsoft 365 for email and documents, LexVault for eDiscovery hosting. The firm has no approved AI tool. Several associates have admitted using public chatbots for first drafts.
