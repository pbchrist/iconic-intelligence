# Briggs v. Cascade Anesthesia Partners (closed)
Date: 2024-10-02

Client: Renee Briggs, certified registered nurse anesthetist (employee side).
Lead: Dana Hollis. Associate: Jordan Okafor.

Facts: Cascade's agreement barred Briggs from practicing within 30 miles of any Cascade facility for 3 years after leaving. She left for a hospital in Everett. Cascade sent a cease-and-desist and threatened an injunction.

Strategy: We argued the covenant was void under Washington's non-compete statute, RCW 49.62. Two points carried it. First, a duration over 18 months is presumed unreasonable under the statute, and Cascade could not rebut it. Second, the 30-mile radius covered every hospital in the region she could realistically work at.

Outcome: Summary judgment for Briggs, September 2024. The court awarded statutory damages of $5,000 plus $41,800 in attorney fees against Cascade.

Lessons: Move early for declaratory judgment instead of waiting for the employer to sue. Get the employee's full compensation history on day one. The fee-shifting provision gave us leverage in every conversation with opposing counsel.
