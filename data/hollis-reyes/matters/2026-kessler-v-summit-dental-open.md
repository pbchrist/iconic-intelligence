# Kessler v. Summit Dental Partners (open)
Date: 2026-10-20

Client: Dr. Alicia Kessler, DDS (employee side). Engaged October 14, 2026.
Lead: Dana Hollis. Associate: Jordan Okafor.

Facts: Dr. Kessler is leaving Summit Dental Partners after 6 years to open her own practice in Kirkland. Her agreement bars her from practicing dentistry within 25 miles of any Summit office for 2 years and from soliciting Summit patients for 2 years. Her 2025 W-2 compensation was $212,000.

Status: Summit's counsel, Graves Whitlock LLP, sent a demand letter on October 27 (see correspondence). Our response is due November 12, 2026.

Open questions: Whether Summit gave her the covenant before acceptance of the offer, as the statute requires. Whether any patients followed her on their own. Her new lease is signed and opening is set for January 5, 2027, so timing matters.
