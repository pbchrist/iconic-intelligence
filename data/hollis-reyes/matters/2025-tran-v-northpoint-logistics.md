# Tran v. Northpoint Logistics (closed)
Date: 2025-06-20

Client: Northpoint Logistics (employer side).
Lead: Marco Reyes.

Facts: Former regional sales director Kevin Tran joined a competitor and contacted Northpoint customers. Northpoint's agreement had a 2-year non-compete and a 2-year customer non-solicit.

Outcome: Settled June 2025. The non-compete was dropped. Tran agreed to a 9-month non-solicit limited to 40 named accounts. Northpoint paid its own fees of about $68,000.

Lessons: The 2-year terms we inherited were unenforceable on their face, and opposing counsel knew it. We have since redrafted Northpoint's template to 12 months with named accounts. For employer clients, overbroad covenants cost leverage rather than adding it.
