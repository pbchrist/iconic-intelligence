# Research memo: enforceability of the Summit covenant
Date: 2026-10-24

To: Dana Hollis. From: Jordan Okafor.

1. Earnings threshold. RCW 49.62 voids non-competes for employees earning below an annually adjusted threshold. Dr. Kessler's $212,000 is well above it, so unlike Briggs, the threshold does not help us.
2. Duration. A non-compete longer than 18 months is presumed unreasonable unless the employer proves by clear and convincing evidence that a longer period is needed. Summit's 2 years triggers the presumption. This is the same argument that won Briggs.
3. Timing of disclosure. If the covenant was not disclosed in writing no later than her acceptance of the offer, it is void. Her offer letter is dated March 2, 2020; the agreement is dated March 30, 2020. We need to confirm whether she saw the covenant before she accepted.
4. Remedies. If we prevail, the statute provides $5,000 in statutory damages plus fees, as in Briggs.

Recommendation: Answer the November 12 letter firmly, cite the 18-month presumption and the disclosure timing, and propose 6 months / 10 miles as our counter.
