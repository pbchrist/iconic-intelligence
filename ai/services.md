# Services and delivery

> ICONIC reference page. Checked against https://iconic.onl/ on 2026-11-16. If this page and the website ever differ, the website is current.

ICONIC finds hidden opportunities to increase revenue, eliminate wasted effort and outperform the competition, then builds the systems to capture them. AI is the mechanism; business performance is the product.

## Three ways to work with ICONIC

**Learn AI: specialized AI training.** Practical training for professionals and teams, specific to the customer's industry, responsibilities and workflows. Audiences: attorneys and law firms, recruiters and staffing firms, automotive dealerships, business owners and executives. Formats and prices: specialized workshop $249 per person, private professional training $1,000 per session, team training $3,000 per team. Details: https://iconic.onl/services/ai-training/

**Evaluate: the ICONIC Evaluation, $1,000.** A paid business and AI opportunity assessment. ICONIC investigates operations, challenges, competitive position and existing systems. Deliverables: business opportunity assessment, identification of high-value opportunities, prioritized recommendations, estimated financial impact where evidence supports it, technical and operational feasibility analysis, recommended implementation roadmap. Details: https://iconic.onl/services/evaluation/

**Build: custom AI implementation, starting at $18,000.** ICONIC designs, develops and deploys specialized AI systems: AI agents, competitive intelligence, revenue and pricing intelligence, workflow automation, business research and monitoring, customer acquisition systems, custom integrations, internal knowledge and decision-support systems, and secure private AI deployments. Each build is scoped individually under a separate agreement. Details: https://iconic.onl/services/build/

**Ongoing intelligence and optimization.** After implementation: monitoring, maintenance, optimization, competitive intelligence and system improvements. Priced to scope. Details: https://iconic.onl/services/ongoing-intelligence/

## How customers start

- Someone who wants to understand AI starts with training.
- Someone with a business problem starts with the $1,000 evaluation.
- Builds are by invitation and begin with an evaluation. Training is not required first.

## Private AI

ICONIC builds and runs private AI systems on its own hardware, and can deploy a system on a computer in the client's office so the client's files are not sent to an AI company. See [architecture](https://iconic.onl/ai/architecture.md) and [security and privacy](https://iconic.onl/ai/security-privacy.md).
