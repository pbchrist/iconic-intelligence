What stays in the system

    The analysis becomes repeatable.
    A one-off answer turns into a capability that runs itself next time.
  4. 04 · Advantage
    You move Monday morning.
    They find out in two weeks. Everything learned goes back into the nucleus.
  5. 05 · Compound
    Then it runs again, faster.
