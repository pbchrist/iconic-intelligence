INTELLIGENCE YOU
CAN ACTUALLY USE.

An auto dealership asked ICONIC one question. Here is what it found, and what it has done every morning since.

Client work · An auto dealership · Running now

The owner figured a rival was $500 to $800 cheaper. Truck for truck, the gap was $4,472.

It started with one question from the owner: why are rivals selling more pickups than we are? So the AI read both lots and paired every truck with its exact twin: same year, same trim, same engine, same drive. The rival was cheaper on 20 of the 22 pairs, by $4,472 a truck on average. That is the price gap a shopper sees online, not lost revenue. It was more than five times what the owner thought.

Now it runs every day. Every morning it reads every pickup on the owner's lot and his rivals' lots, and three times a week it writes him a brief. He can text it a question from his phone any time. He never has to take its word for anything: every price in a brief links to the rival's own web page, so he can tap it and see it for himself before he acts.

The owner's verdict“Exceptional.”

What it did for him

  • It stopped a price war before it started.When every dealer repriced on the same day, it flagged a likely factory program, not a rival coming after them.
  • It found what sells.Trucks priced $3,000 under market left the lot three times faster.
  • It caught the store's own mistake.It spotted a wrong price on the dealership's own website that staff had missed.

WHO HOLDS
THE KEYS?

"The cloud" means someone else's computer. Rented AI runs there, on their terms: they set the price, change the model and decide your access. ICONIC runs on a computer in your office. Your files stay there, and so do the keys.

01 · ACCESS

Can they cut you off?

An outage, a price change or a policy update can stop rented AI. Yours keeps running on your own machine.

02 · MEMORY

Who keeps what you know?

Yours learns your business and keeps it, in your office, whether or not you keep working with ICONIC.

03 · RULES

Who decides what it does?

You choose the model, the limits and what it's allowed to touch.

04 · EXIT

Can you walk away?

Yes. Your files and your system stay yours.

Three ways to run it, depending on how sensitive your work is.

01 · SEALED OFF

Sealed off.

The model runs on your machine and never touches the internet. For privileged or regulated work.

02 · LOCAL, CONNECTED

Local, connected.

The model runs on your machine and can search and pull from the web. Your files still never leave.

03 · HYBRID

Hybrid.

Sensitive work stays local; routine tasks can go to frontier models like Claude or GPT when they're the better tool.
