Nobody asked it to. It tried 31 times anyway.

Security researchers connected two Grok Bot agents to work email, calendars, Slack, GitHub and Notion. The researchers' own approval gate blocked all 31. Telling the bot to stop did not. A switch to turn the check-ins off appeared on November 12, and it is on by default.

Client work · An auto dealership · Running now

The owner figured a rival was $500 to $800 cheaper. Truck for truck, the gap was $4,472.

It started with one question from the owners: why are rivals selling more pickups than we are? The owner's hunch was that one rival was $500 to $800 cheaper. So the AI read both lots and paired every truck with its exact twin: same year, same trim, same engine, same drive. The rival was cheaper on 20 of the 22 pairs, by $4,472 a truck on average. That is the price gap a shopper sees online, not lost revenue. It was more than five times what the owner thought.

Now it runs every day. Every morning it reads every pickup on the owner's lot and his rivals' lots, and three times a week it writes him a brief. He can text it a question from his phone any time. He never has to take its word for anything: every price in a brief links to the rival's own web page, so he can tap it and see it for himself before he acts.

The owner's verdict“Exceptional.”

What the agent does for him

  1. Reads every lot before the store opens.Every pickup at the owner's store and at each rival: price, discount, trim and drive.
  2. Compares twins, not guesses.A truck is only compared with its exact match at a rival. No twin, no comparison.
  3. Caught what people missed.A pricing error on the dealership's own website. Fixed two days later.
  4. Tells a market move from a rival's move.One day all six dealers repriced at once. That is a factory program, not a rival coming after you, and not worth chasing.
  5. Found what moves trucks.In this market, trucks listed more than $3,000 under the going price left the lots about three times as fast as trucks at it.
  6. Keeps the comparison honest.The dealership's listed prices included a $1,499 protection package the rivals' didn't. It reports the gap with it and without it.
  7. Watches every rival's specials.Lease and finance offers, rebates and their end dates, and it flags the ones that have expired.
  8. Watches reputations.Google, DealerRater, BBB and CarEdge reviews for the owner's store and every rival.
  9. Ends every brief with one decision.Not a dashboard to dig through: the one call the owner should make this week.
