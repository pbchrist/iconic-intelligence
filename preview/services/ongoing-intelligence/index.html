QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

What does ongoing intelligence cost?

It is priced to the scope and service level you need, so there is no published rate. You receive a written scope and price before anything starts.

Do I have to sign up for ongoing support after a build?

No. A build is handed over as a working system. Ongoing support is optional.

Can ICONIC look after a system someone else built?

Possibly. It depends on how the system was built and what access is available. An evaluation is usually the right first step.

What does “competitive intelligence” mean here?

A system that keeps watching the things that affect your business, such as competitor prices, listings or market changes, and tells you when something moves, with the evidence attached.
