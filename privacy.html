Privacy

Last updated November 1, 2026

ICONIC is run by Patrick Christell in Seattle, Washington. This page explains what this website collects and what happens to it. The short version: very little, and nothing is sold.

What we collect

  • When you book a call. Booking runs through Calendly, which collects the name, email and any notes you give it. Calendly's own privacy policy covers how it handles that. I use it only to hold the call and follow up with you.
  • When you email or call. I keep your message and contact details to reply and to work with you.
  • When you use the live demo. Your questions are answered by an AI running on my own computer, not by an AI company. Questions may appear briefly in the demo's public activity log, and the demo keeps anonymous counts of conversations and of connections it learns. Your network address is used only to limit how many questions can be asked per hour. Please don't type personal or confidential information into the demo.
  • Basic web logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' network addresses for security and operations.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics that profile you.
  • No selling or renting of personal information, ever.
  • Nothing you type into the demo is sent to an AI company.

Downloads

The one-page guide is a free download. No email address is required.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email patrick@iconic.onl and I'll handle it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
