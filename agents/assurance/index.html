Can your AI agent prove what it did?

An agent can produce a convincing answer while misreading evidence, overstepping tool permissions, or confusing a proposed action with a completed one. ICONIC Agent Assurance is designed to evaluate these failure modes with reproducible cases and source-linked findings.
