04 · How agent purchases work
Discover → Evaluate → Authorize → Receive


01 DiscoverCall marketplace_list_offers to inspect all nine offers and see which three are purchasable.
02 EvaluateCall marketplace_quote and marketplace_preflight. Live fixed-price quotes are available only for the three active offers; other services require a custom inquiry.
03 AuthorizeAuthorize payment via a compatible agent wallet; approval and a Stripe SPT credential are required for real charges.
04 DeliverOnly purchasable offers may proceed to payment. The three purchasable products use guarded automated fulfillment; the six custom engagements have no checkout or automatic delivery.


Payments, buyer consent, evidence quality, and customer data access are separate safeguards. The live nine-offer MPP gateway currently marks three offers purchasable; a payment challenge alone never means a successful charge. MPP SPT is distinct from ChatGPT Wallet. An end-to-end Wallet purchase has not been independently verified. An agent does not need the human's raw credit-card details.
