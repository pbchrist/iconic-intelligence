01 / THE MEASUREMENT

Discoverable is a start.
Chosen is the test.

Directory checks answer whether a tool can be found and connected. Our evaluation examines a different question: what gets selected when the agent has alternatives?

  1. 01 / SCOPE

    DEFINE THE COMPARISON

    Agree on the publisher's tools, the tasks that matter, and a credible set of alternatives. We don't manufacture a favorable comparison.

  2. 02 / MEASURE

    OBSERVE THE CHOICE

    Evaluate tool selection under controlled conditions. Report observed choices, the evaluation scope, and meaningful uncertainty.

  3. 03 / DIAGNOSE

    IDENTIFY THE FRICTION

    Find where other tools are selected instead and what a publisher can reasonably change to improve its presentation.

  4. 04 / VALIDATE

    RETEST THE CHANGE

    Evaluate targeted improvements against a defined baseline. Report gains, losses, and inconclusive results without inventing a win.

02 / THE DELIVERABLE

Evidence a publisher
can act on.

A decision-ready assessment of selection performance, actionable improvements, and what changed after testing them.

BASELINE

Are you being chosen?

Selection results under defined comparison conditions, with transparent scope and limitations.

DIAGNOSIS

Where are you losing?

Alternative tools selected and a prioritized explanation of changes worth investigating.

VALIDATION

Did the change work?

Before-and-after evidence on the improvements evaluated, including when outcomes do not improve.

Measurement boundary: This is a controlled comparison, not monitoring of organic ChatGPT recommendations, directory ranking, installs, customer traffic, or guaranteed adoption. Tool capabilities are screened for comparison; they are not certified as identical. This selection evaluation does not execute the candidate tools.
