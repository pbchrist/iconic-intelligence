08 · FAQ

Straight
answers.

What is MCP, in plain English?

The Model Context Protocol is the open standard that lets AI assistants like Claude, Claude Code and Cursor use outside tools. Think of it as a universal plug: you give your assistant one web address, it sees the tools behind it, and it decides when to use them. You don't write code.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. The public endpoint has no sign-in. In Claude, choose No sign in when you add the connector. If a client asks for OAuth details, leave them empty.

What does it cost?

Connecting, the assessment, the synthetic demo and the sandbox are free. A Reserve Pass, your own private Reserve for 30 days, is $19 one-time, bought here by card or by your agent. A Reserve wired into your own systems is a scoped engagement that starts with the free 30-minute check.

Does it keep what I type?

The assessment answers once and stores nothing; its receipt says record_retention: NONE_PUBLIC_SERVICE. Sandbox records are fictional and erased after an hour. The server keeps only anonymous counts of which tools were used.

Why does the address end in ts.net?

The server runs on ICONIC's own hardware in Seattle and is published to the internet through Tailscale. Same machine-first idea as the product: your intelligence on hardware you control.

Is it a real listing or just a page?

It's in the official MCP Registry as io.github.pbchrist/iconic-intelligence-reserve, and an independent test client connects to it and calls its tools every day.

How do I get a Reserve on my own business?

Book the free check. We pick one recurring decision, connect only the records you approve, and measure whether your agents get better answers with the history than without it.
